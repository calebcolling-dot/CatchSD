CastSD

Quiet dawn on a prairie reservoir.

A South Dakota fishing companion: public waters on a map, species intel written like a field notebook, and a mark-based identifier you can use with wet hands.

Waters and notes are a curated field set, not the complete GFP inventory. Confirm seasons, limits, and access in the current handbook at gfp.sd.gov.

Add CastSD to your home screen from the browser share menu for a full-screen app. No account. No ads. Map tiles need a connection; the guide and identifier stay on the device after the first visit.
